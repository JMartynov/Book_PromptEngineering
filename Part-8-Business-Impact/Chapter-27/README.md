## Key Takeaways:
- Moving from "Chatting" to **ROI Calculation**.
- The 3 dimensions: **Labor Displacement**, **Engineering Overhead**, and **Inference Costs**.
- Deep technical dive into the **ROI Equation**, **Error Cost Attribution**, and **Break-Even Analysis**.
- 8 real-world Python examples illustrating:
  - Standardized **ROI Calculation** functions.
  - **Labor Savings** estimations based on task volume.
  - Tracking **Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)** including engineering hours.
  - **Model ROI Benchmarking** (Accuracy vs. Price).
  - Calculating the **True Cost of Hallucinations**.
  - Decision logic for **Prompt Optimization ROI**.
  - Automated **Live ROI Dashboards**.
  - **Scaling Analysis** and break-even point projection.
